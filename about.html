About Lxwyer Up

Building India's Legal Future

Our Mission

Lxwyer Up is building India's first comprehensive legal operating system — starting with a powerful AI litigation workspace and CRM designed specifically for how Indian lawyers actually practice law.

Every day, thousands of Indian lawyers struggle with fragmented tools, scattered case files, missed court dates, and the overwhelming burden of managing a legal practice. We're here to change that.

Why We Exist

Indian lawyers spend more time managing their practice than actually practicing law. Between tracking court dates across multiple courts, managing client communications, drafting documents, and keeping up with legal research — the administrative burden is crushing.

Lxwyer Up was born from this frustration. We interviewed over 300 lawyers and judges across India to understand the real pain points of legal practice. The result is a platform that finally puts everything in one place.

What We're Building

  • AI-Powered Case Management: Track all your cases, court dates, hearings, and deadlines in one intelligent dashboard.
  • Legal Research AI: An AI assistant trained on Indian case law that helps you research case precedents, draft arguments, and prepare for hearings.
  • Client CRM: Manage client relationships, communications, documents, and billing from a single interface.
  • Court Date Tracker: Automated reminders and updates for all your court appearances across multiple jurisdictions.
  • Document Drafting: AI-assisted legal document drafting that understands Indian legal formats and requirements.

Built for India

Unlike foreign legal tech solutions that are retrofitted for the Indian market, Lxwyer Up is built from the ground up for Indian courts, Indian legal procedures, and Indian lawyers. From district courts to the Supreme Court, from solo practitioners to large law firms — we serve the entire legal ecosystem.

Our Promise

The first 500 founding members will receive permanent premium access and a voice in shaping the future of legal technology in India. We're not just building software — we're building a community of forward-thinking legal professionals who believe that technology can transform how justice is delivered in India.
